### Key Gestures

1. **Place Component**
   - Drag components from **ComponentToolbar** to **SchematicView**.
   - Click to rotate components.
   - Right-click component to **Edit value**.

2. **Wire Pin-to-Pin**
   - Click on a pin in **SchematicView** and drag to another pin.
   - Drag to place wire, click to connect.

3. **Right-click Edit Value**
   - Right-click on a component or wire in **SchematicView** to edit its value.

4. **R Rotate Component**
   - Right-click on a component and select **R Rotate**.

5. **Del Component**
   - Select a component in **SchematicView** and press **Del**.

6. **Ctrl+Z/Y Undo**
   - Press **Ctrl+Z** for undo, **Ctrl+Y** for redo.

7. **Wheel Zoom**
   - Scroll wheel to zoom in/out.

8. **Middle-Drag Pan**
   - Hold **middle-drag** to pan the **SchematicView**.

### Trade-offs

- **Complexity**: The schematic editor is more complex than the rest of the application due to the need to handle component placement, wiring, and pin connections.
- **Performance**: Real-time updates and undo/redo operations are implemented using the CadEngine, which may introduce some overhead, especially with complex circuits.
- **User Experience**: The initial setup is a bit more complex for users accustomed to LTspice, but the familiar interface and drag-and-drop capabilities should make it easier for them to get started.
- **Scalability**: The application is designed to be scalable, but the initial implementation may not be as efficient as more advanced EDA tools.
